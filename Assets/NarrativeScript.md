# Break The Curse - Narrative Script

## Logline
A lone wanderer wakes in a kingdom split between a dying human realm and a hungry demon world. To break the curse, they must recover the kingdom's lost crystals, survive the creatures shaped by the darkness, and choose to carry the last light through the final portal.

## Opening Narration
The kingdom did not fall in a single night.

It faded. First the bells went quiet. Then the roads forgot the feet that crossed them. Then the sky split open, and the demon world began to breathe through the cracks.

You wake where both worlds touch. No crown, no army, no memory strong enough to trust. Only the pull of crystal light ahead, and the curse behind you.

If any soul can still break this darkness, it has to be one willing to walk through it.

## Tutorial Lines
1. Wake up, wanderer. The curse is close, but your legs still remember the road.
2. Move through the ruins. Every step forward keeps the darkness from swallowing you whole.
3. Jump across the broken stone. The kingdom left paths for those brave enough to look up.
4. Crystals hold fragments of the old light. Collect them before the demon world consumes them.
5. Press onward. The first portal will only answer when enough light has returned to your hands.

## Level 1 - Haunted Caverns
The caverns were once safe roads beneath the kingdom. Merchants crossed here with lanterns, songs, and bright crystal charms. Now the walls whisper in voices that are almost human.

Each crystal you find remembers a piece of the world before the curse.

When the flying eye appears:
The curse has learned to hunt from above. Do not let it carry you into the dark.

When the portal unlocks:
The crystal light gathers at the gate. The portal opens, but what waits beyond is less human than the road behind.

## Level 2 - The Inverted Road
The demon world is not another place. It is the same kingdom turned inside out.

Here, every safe path has a shadow. Every shadow has teeth. Use the inversion carefully. Stay too long, and the curse will send its orb to drag you back.

When the orb starts chasing:
The demon world has noticed you. Run. The longer you remain here, the faster it remembers your name.

When enough crystals are collected:
The light does not destroy the darkness. It reveals the doorway hidden inside it.

## Level 3 - Final Ascent
Above the ruins, the curse thins into ash and wind. The last portal waits where the two worlds tear against each other.

The creatures here are not guarding the exit. They are afraid you will reach it.

Before the final portal:
The curse promised the kingdom endless life and gave it endless fear. Step through with the crystals, and choose an ending it cannot control.

## Ending Narration
Light returns slowly.

Not as a sunrise. Not as victory shouted from towers. It returns in small things: a bell ringing again, a road remembering its name, a sky no longer split by hunger.

You escaped the curse, but more than that, you carried proof that it could be broken.

The kingdom is wounded. The darkness is not forgotten.

But the last light survived.
